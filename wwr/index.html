Why Raft with Trancura?

Certified Expert Guides

Our internationally certified guides prioritize your safety while maximizing the fun. With extensive local knowledge and emergency training, they ensure an unforgettable and secure adventure on every rapid.

Top-Tier Equipment

We invest in the best: self-bailing rafts, impact-resistant helmets, and buoyancy aids that exceed safety standards. All gear is inspected before every trip—so you focus on the thrill, not the gear.

Unmatched Natural Beauty

Paddle through pristine Patagonian landscapes: crystal-clear waters, native forests, and snow-capped Andes peaks. Our routes blend adrenaline with awe-inspiring scenery you won't find anywhere else.

Adventures for Everyone

First timer or seasoned pro? We tailor trips to your comfort level—from calm family floats to heart-pounding Class V rapids. Private groups, corporate events, and custom itineraries welcome.
